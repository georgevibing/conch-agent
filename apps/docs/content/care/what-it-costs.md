Conch shows cost the way your provider charges:

- **A key**: money, pay as you go.
- **A plan**, like Claude Max or ChatGPT Plus: how much of it you've used.
- **This computer**: free.

## What a reply cost

Point at a reply: its cost is beside **Copy**, as "$0.04" or "Plan". On a phone it's always shown. Tap it for:

- what the cache saved;
- tokens read and written;
- on a plan, how much is used, like "Current session: 42% used".

Conch uses the provider's price, else the list price. If it doesn't know a price, it says so rather than guess.

## What a chat cost

Once a chat spends money, a chip after **Model · Mode** under the message box shows the total: "$0.31". Tap it, or press <kbd>mod+k</kbd> then **What this chat spent**, to see the spending, including tasks started from the chat, and what the cache saved.

## A limit for one chat

1. Tap the chat's chip.
2. Under **Limit for this chat**, enter an amount, like 2.
3. Press **Set limit**.

At the limit, your message waits, or a reply stops part way, until you choose:

- **Raise to $5**: your message goes.
- **Use Gemma 3 on this computer** (or a plan model, or a cheaper one): carry on for less. A cheaper model on the same key gets a little more room, shown on the button.
- **Stop here**: the message stays unanswered.

To remove a limit, clear the amount and press **Remove limit**.

## A budget for the month

Set a **Monthly budget** in **Settings → Usage**. It counts every chat's pay-as-you-go spending, whatever your default provider.

- Past 80%, a chat says so in one line, once a month.
- At the budget, a chat asks before spending more, with the same choices. Chats on a plan or this computer carry on.

Routines have [their own limit](../features/routines.md#it-wont-run-up-a-bill), and count toward the month.

## Before a model makes replies cost more

Switch to a much pricier model on a long chat, and one line warns you: "With a chat this long, each reply from Opus 5.5 costs about $0.84. The last one cost $0.21." Pick the old one to go back. It only shows when a reply would cost at least 25¢ and twice the last.

## Good to know

- Only you can change a limit, never the assistant or a task.
- Chats from a chat app, like Telegram, are counted but never held at a limit, since nobody is there to tap.
- Prices are list prices, so your bill can differ a little.
