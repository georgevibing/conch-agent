Conch says what your chats cost the way your provider charges you. On a key you pay as you go, that's money. On a plan, like Claude Max or ChatGPT Plus, it's how much of the plan is used. A model on this computer is free.

## What a reply cost

Point at a reply, and its cost is beside **Copy**: "$0.04", or "Plan" on a subscription. On a phone it's always there. Tap it to see more:

- what reading from the cache saved;
- how many tokens it read and wrote;
- on a plan, how much of it is used, like "Current session: 42% used".

The provider's own price is used when it gives one. Otherwise Conch works it out from the list price. If it doesn't know a model's price, it says so instead of guessing.

## What a chat cost

Once a chat has spent money, a small chip beside the model picker shows the total: "$0.31". Tap it to see what was spent, including the tasks started from that chat, and what the cache saved. You can also press <kbd>mod+k</kbd> and type **What this chat spent**.

## A limit for one chat

1. Tap the chat's chip.
2. Under **Limit for this chat**, enter an amount, like 2.
3. Press **Set limit**.

At the limit, your message waits, and the chat asks what to do. If a reply reaches it part way, the reply stops and carries on once you choose.

- **Raise to $5** raises the limit, and your message goes.
- **Use Gemma 3 on this computer** (or a model on your plan, or a cheaper one) carries on with a model that costs less. A cheaper model on the same key gets a little more room, and the button says how much.
- **Stop here** leaves the message unanswered.

To take a limit off, clear the amount and press **Remove limit**.

## A budget for the month

Set a **Monthly budget** in **Settings → Usage**. It counts what every chat spends on pay-as-you-go providers, whichever provider is your default.

- When the month passes 80% of it, the chat says so in one line. You hear it once a month.
- At the budget, a chat asks before spending more, with the same choices. Chats on a plan or on this computer carry on.

Routines have [a limit of their own](../features/routines.md#it-wont-run-up-a-bill). Their spending counts toward the month too.

## Before a model makes replies cost more

When you pick a model that costs a lot more on a long chat, one line says what a reply will cost: "With a chat this long, each reply from Opus 5.5 costs about $0.84. The last one cost $0.21." Pick the one you had to go back. Conch only says it when it's at least 25¢ a reply and twice what the last reply cost.

## Good to know

- Only you can change a limit. The assistant can't raise one, and neither can a task.
- Chats from a chat app, like Telegram, are counted but never left waiting at a limit, because there's nobody there to tap the card.
- Prices are list prices, so your provider's bill can be a little different.
