Your assistant has a name, a face, a way of talking, and things it always does. You can make more than one: a calm planner for trips, a blunt reviewer for your code, a patient tutor for your daughter's homework. Each one is an **agent**.

Every agent is the same Conch underneath. They all know what Conch remembers about you, use the same apps, skills and providers, and follow the same safety settings. Only how they talk and what they focus on changes.

## The one you start with

Setting up Conch makes your first agent, with the name and personality you chose. If you never make another, nothing else changes.

## Make another

In **Settings → Agents**, press **New agent**, then give it:

- **A name.** It's shown beside every reply, and it's what the agent calls itself.
- **A face.** One of Conch's pictures, a picture of your own, or one made for you from a few words, when a provider that makes pictures is connected.
- **What it's for.** One line, like "Plans trips and keeps the bookings". It shows when you choose an agent.
- **A tone and a personality.** Warm, concise, playful, precise, calm, formal or candid, and anything else in your own words.
- **Instructions.** What it should always do: "Answer in British English", "Give me two options, never one".

It can also start new chats with a model and a [permission mode](../reference/modes.md) of its own. Full trust is never one of them: you choose that for a chat yourself.

A picture of your own is kept without what a camera writes into it, like where and when it was taken.

## Choose who answers

A new chat starts with your **default** agent. Choose another before you send the first message, or type `/agent` and a name.

You can change who answers in the middle of a chat. The chat shows where it changed, and the new agent carries on from what was said, in its own voice.

Background [tasks](tasks.md) are done by the agent of the chat they came from. A [routine](routines.md) can be given an agent of its own.

## In chat apps

Each chat app you [talk to Conch from](../channels/telegram.md) can have its own agent. Send `/agent` there to see your agents and choose one, or `/agent sage` to choose by name.

## What an agent can't change

An agent's personality and instructions shape how it talks. They can't lift a permission, turn off a safety check, or make it skip asking you. Conch's own rules come first, whatever an agent's instructions say, and the assistant can't edit an agent itself.
