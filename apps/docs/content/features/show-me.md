Some answers aren't a paragraph. Ask for a tip calculator, a chart or a trip plan, and it opens beside the chat, to use, change and come back to.

## Ask for one

Say what you want: "chart my spending by month". Your assistant picks the kind:

- **Page**: a small app, or a page you can use.
- **Document**: something to read and keep.
- **Chart** and **Table**: your numbers, drawn or in rows.
- **Diagram** and **Picture**: how something works, or a drawing.

A card appears in the chat and the panel opens beside it (over the chat on a phone). Close the panel when you're done; **Open** on the card brings it back. Charts, tables, diagrams and pictures show a preview on their card.

## Use it and change it

The panel has three tabs: **View** (**Use** for a page), **Code**, and **Changes** from the version before.

To change it, ask in the chat: "add a total row". Each change is a new version, and the version picker goes back to any of them. Conch keeps up to 30, always including the first.

At the top are **Copy**, **Download**, **Pin as an app** and **Full screen**, with **Delete…** under **More**. A chart switches between **Chart** and **Table**. A table sorts by any heading.

## Change it yourself

Press **Edit**. The code opens beside the result, which changes as you type. On a phone, or in a narrow panel, switch between **Edit** and **Preview**.

- If something doesn't fit, it says where: "Line 3 has 3 values, but the header has 2." **Save** waits until it's fixed.
- **Undo** and **Redo** are beside the code. <kbd>mod+s</kbd> saves, <kbd>esc</kbd> cancels.
- What you save is a new version marked **Edited by you**. Your assistant's next change starts from it.
- An unsaved edit waits if you close the panel or switch chats. **Cancel** asks before throwing it away.
- If a newer version came in while you edited, **Save mine as the newest** keeps yours.

## Live data

A page can show live numbers, like the weather or whether a build passed. The first time it reads a site, Conch asks: **Let "Weather now" read live data from api.open-meteo.com?**

- It reads without your cookies or sign-ins, and fills blanks in an address only from values it named in advance.
- You're asked once per page and site, and again if a new version reads a different address.
- A site on this computer needs a second yes: **Let it read from this computer**.
- The bar above says **Live · Updated 2 min ago**. **Update now** reads again. If a site doesn't answer, the page keeps what it had.
- **Reads from** lists the sites, and **Stop** takes one back. All are in **Settings → Security → Advanced → Live data in pages**.

## Pin it as an app

**Pin as an app** puts it under **Apps** at the top of the sidebar, as a tile with its own page, and it keeps its live data. Right-click or press and hold the tile to unpin it. Extra apps are behind **All apps**.

**Refresh** has your assistant get fresh data and make a new version, in a chat that can only update that one thing. **Watch it refresh** opens that chat.

## Pages run sealed off

A page is code your assistant wrote, perhaps after reading a page you don't trust. So, while you use it or edit it:

- It can't see your cookies, sign-ins or anything else in Conch.
- It can't load from or send to the internet. It reads only through Conch, from sites you allowed.
- It can't take you to another site. Conch shows a link's address and asks first.

A page with links or code that could take you elsewhere opens with its code off, until you press **Run it anyway**. Documents, charts, tables, diagrams and pictures are drawn by Conch and load nothing from other sites. More in [Signing in and staying safe](../security/signing-in.md).

## Good to know

- <kbd>mod+k</kbd> finds anything made for you by name. See [Find anything](./find.md).
- **Activity** in the sidebar has a **Made** filter.
- Deleting one removes every version, not the chat.
- They're in your [backups](../care/backups.md), with your chats.
