**Effective 11 October 2026.**

This policy covers conchagent.com: the front page and the documentation (together, "the website"). The maintainer of Conch ("we", "us") runs the website.

Conch itself, the software you install, runs on your own computer and isn't covered here. Its [security documentation](../security/signing-in.md) describes what it does with your data.

## The short version

- We set no cookies.
- We run no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
- We load nothing from other companies: no third-party scripts, fonts or images.
- We have no accounts, forms or newsletters, so we never ask you for anything.
- We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't collect any.

## What we collect

Nothing. The website is a set of static pages. It doesn't record who you are, what you read or where you came from.

## Your browser's own storage

If you choose light or dark mode, your browser keeps that choice in its local storage, on your device. It's never sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings.

## Hosting

[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com) hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details your browser sends with each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page's address.

- GitHub uses them to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.
- It may keep them in logs for a limited time.
- It handles them as described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), and may process them in many countries.

We don't receive these details, and we haven't turned on any analytics product.

## Links to other sites

The website links to other sites, such as GitHub, where Conch's code and downloads live. The install commands on this website fetch Conch from GitHub. Those sites' own privacy policies apply when you visit or download from them.

## Children

The website isn't directed at children. We collect nothing, so we hold no information about anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights over your personal data: to access it, correct it, delete it or object to its use.

- We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give, change or delete.
- For the request details GitHub processes, contact GitHub.
- You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to change this policy at any time. Changes take effect when published on this page, with a new effective date. Every earlier version stays public in the website's [history on GitHub](https://github.com/georgevibing/conch-agent/commits/main/apps/docs/content/legal/privacy.md). If you keep using the website after a change, you accept the new policy.

## Contact

Send questions about this policy to the maintainer by [opening an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/georgevibing/conch-agent/issues).
