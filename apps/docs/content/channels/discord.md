## Connect it

Open **Apps → Talk to me here → Discord**.

1. **Make a Discord app.** In the [Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications), press **New Application**, name it after your assistant, tick the box and press **Create**.
2. **Copy its token.** Open **Bot**, press **Reset Token**, confirm, then **Copy**, and paste it on Conch's page. Discord shows it once. Lost it? Press **Reset Token** again.
3. **Add it to your server** with the invite on Conch's page. Discord only lets you message a bot you share a server with. The bot gets no permissions there.
4. **Say hello.** Send your bot a private message. When Conch asks **Is this you?**, press **That's me**.

No server? In Discord, press **+** in the server list, then **Create My Own → For me and my friends**.

## Good to know

- **No portal settings** to switch on.
- **Conch's commands are in Discord's `/` menu**, with a box for what follows. Only you see what you chose. See [Slash commands](../reference/slash-commands.md#in-chat-apps).
- **While it works** you see "typing…".
- **Pictures and files** arrive as attachments: up to ten a message and 10 MB together.
- **Server channels** answer mentions once you turn them on. See [In a group](index.md#in-a-group).
