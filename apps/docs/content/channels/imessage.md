## Connect it

Text yourself from your iPhone, and your assistant answers in the same chat, through Messages on your Mac. Open **Apps → Talk to me here → iMessage**.

1. **Let Conch read Messages.** Press **Open System Settings** and turn on the app Conch names under **Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access** (Terminal, iTerm, or `node` in the background). The step ticks itself off.
2. **Choose how you'll text.** Pick **I text myself** for a Mac signed in with your own Apple ID, or **This Mac has its own Apple ID** for a Mac people text at its own address. Conch shows the address Messages uses.
3. **Say hello.** Texting yourself, you're in: Conch sends you a hello. On a Mac with its own Apple ID, text it from your iPhone, then press **That's me**.

## Good to know

- **Only the chat with yourself is read.** Never your other conversations or groups.
- **On a Mac with its own Apple ID**, **A number just for Conch** is on. Anyone else gets one polite reply and waits for you to **Let them in** or **Block**.
- **Approvals are a number.** Reply with the answer's number, or yes, or no.
- **macOS asks once** whether Conch may use Messages. Press **Allow**. If you said no, the channel page shows **Open System Settings** (**Privacy & Security → Automation**).
- **iPhone photos** (HEIC) arrive as JPEG, so every provider can see them.
- **Pictures and files come back** after the words. Messages sends them from a **Conch outbox** folder in your Pictures, which empties itself after a few minutes.
- **Your Mac has to be on**, with Conch running. Texts sent while it's off are answered when it's back, for up to a day.
