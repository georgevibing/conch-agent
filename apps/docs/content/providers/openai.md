## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **OpenAI**.
2. Press **Open platform.openai.com** and create a project key. It starts with `sk-proj-`.
3. Paste it.

Or paste it anywhere on **Settings → Providers**.

## What you get

OpenAI’s GPT models your key can use, newest first, billed per use to your organization.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **This is not your ChatGPT plan.** To use that, connect [Codex](codex-cli.md).
- **An admin key (`sk-admin-`) can’t chat.** Make a project key.
