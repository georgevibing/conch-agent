## Connect it

- **Sign in through the Ollama app**, with no key. If Ollama is on this computer, press **Sign in with Ollama** and sign in on the page that opens.
- **Use a key.** Press **Open ollama.com**, create a key and paste it.

## What you get

Big open models on Ollama’s servers, for when this computer is too small to run them.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Not offline.** For a model that stays here, use [On this computer](ollama.md).
- **Plans are usage credits.** The free plan includes some. Your Ollama account shows usage.
