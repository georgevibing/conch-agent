## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **Google Gemini**.
2. Press **Open aistudio.google.com** and create a key. It’s free and starts with `AIza` or `AQ.`.
3. Paste it.

Or paste it anywhere on **Settings → Providers**.

## What you get

Google’s Gemini models: very long context, and good with pictures. The free tier needs no card. A paid key raises the limits.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Free use may train Google’s products.** Paid use doesn’t.
- **Prefer to sign in with your Google account?** Connect [Gemini CLI](gemini-cli.md) instead.
