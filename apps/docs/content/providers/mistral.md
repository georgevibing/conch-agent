## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **Mistral**.
2. Press **Open console.mistral.ai** and create a key. The free plan needs no card.
3. Paste it.

## What you get

Mistral’s models from Europe, good all-round, one name each.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Mistral keys have no prefix.** Paste yours on Mistral’s page, or choose **Mistral** when Conch asks.
