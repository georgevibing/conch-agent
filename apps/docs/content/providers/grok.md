## Connect your Grok plan

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **Grok**.
2. If it isn’t here, press **Install Grok**.
3. Press **Sign in with X**, choose **Copy code and open sign-in page**, paste the code on xAI’s page and approve.

Your SuperGrok or X Premium+ usage applies, through xAI’s own Grok Build program.

Conch's own tools do the work, with its approvals, skill limits and **Undo**. See [Conch's tools, through your plan](index.md#conchs-tools-through-your-plan).

## Good to know

- **One weekly pool,** shared with your other Grok apps.
- **Prefer to pay per use?** Connect the [xAI API](xai.md) with a key.
- **Early support.** Grok Build’s agent mode is still new.
