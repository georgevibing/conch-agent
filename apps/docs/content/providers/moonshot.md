## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **Kimi**.
2. Press **Open platform.kimi.ai**, top up at least $1 and create a key.
3. Paste it.

Or paste it anywhere on **Settings → Providers**, and say whose it is when Conch asks.

Keys from platform.kimi.ai and platform.kimi.com don’t mix. Conch tries the international one, then China, and remembers which works.

## What you get

Moonshot’s Kimi models: very long context, strong at coding and agent work.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Pay-as-you-go keys only.** The Kimi Code plan is for the coding tools Moonshot lists.
