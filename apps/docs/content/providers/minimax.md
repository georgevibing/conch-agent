## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **MiniMax**.
2. Press **Open platform.minimax.io** and create a key, or use your M Plan key.
3. Paste it.

Or paste it anywhere on **Settings → Providers**, and say whose it is when Conch asks.

International and China keys don’t mix. Conch tries both and remembers which works.

## What you get

MiniMax’s models: strong at agent and coding work, with a million-token context. Your M Plan key works here.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Its thinking shows as thinking.** MiniMax thinks out loud inside its answer, and Conch shows that apart.
