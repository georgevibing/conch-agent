## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Providers** and choose **Z.ai**.
2. Press **Open z.ai** and create a key. A key from BigModel in China works too.
3. Paste it.

Or paste it anywhere on **Settings → Providers**, and say whose it is when Conch asks.

Conch tries Z.ai’s international address, then BigModel’s, and remembers which works.

## What you get

Zhipu’s GLM models: strong at coding and very cheap.

Conch runs the chat and supplies the tools: your [apps](../features/apps.md), [memory](../features/memory.md), [skills](../features/skills.md), the [browser](../features/browser.md), files with **Undo** and sealed commands. Anything that changes asks first. Models without tools show **Chat only**. Spend counts toward your [budget](../care/offline.md).

## Good to know

- **Pay-as-you-go keys only.** The GLM Coding Plan is limited to the coding tools Z.ai lists.
