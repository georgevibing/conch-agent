Add a company's API, a model at work or a service a friend uses. It joins the model picker and **Settings → Providers** like the others.

## Make one with Conch

1. Open **Settings → Providers**. Under **Add your own**, type its name in **Which provider?**, like “Fireworks AI”, and press **Make it with Conch**. Or say “add Fireworks as a provider” in any chat.
2. Conch reads the company's API documentation and makes it, usually with no code.
3. Paste your key into the card under the reply and press **Test it**. The provider answers one short line.
4. Press **Add** with its name, like **Add Fireworks AI**.

The assistant never sees your key.

## Any OpenAI-compatible address

Choose **Any OpenAI-compatible address** under **Add your own**. It works like [Another server](servers.md): type the address, and Conch says what answered.

## Add one from a link

Choose **Add from a link** and paste the GitHub address or a `.conchapp` file's address. Conch shows what it is, who made it and where its key goes. Paste your key, press **Test it**, then **Add**.

## Good to know

- **It's a Conch app,** in **Apps**, marked **Made by you** or **Added from a link**. Change it, share it or take it away there. Taking it away takes its key too.
- **Its key goes only to the sites its card shows,** over https, never to this computer or your network.
- **Spending counts.** Prices on its card count toward a chat's spend and your monthly budget.
- **Code only when it must.** A company with its own chat shape gets a little code, sealed off: no files, no programs, only the sites on its card.
- **When it stops working,** **Repair everything** offers **Ask Conch to fix it**. Conch shows a new card, and nothing changes until you press **Update**.
- Every field is in [The app manifest](../reference/app-manifest.md).
