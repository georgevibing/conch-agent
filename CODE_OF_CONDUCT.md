# Code of conduct

Conch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the
work rather than the person. Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised
language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information are not
acceptable anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the
maintainers with GitHub's **Report content** option on the issue, pull request or
comment. Reports are read promptly and kept confidential. The maintainers may
edit or remove content, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, as
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
